Different journeys · one design rule

Record every path at the moment it happens, keep its cause attached, and give each person the lens they need to understand, replay, compare, or challenge the same evidence.

1 Capture the real journey Backend runs, agent decisions, app actions, and visual explorations record themselves while they happen.
2 Keep the cause attached Every result stays connected to the reads, context, choices, people, and agents that produced it.
3 Choose the right lens Engineers can debug the same evidence that people inspect, replay, compare, and challenge.
The journey stays the same. The lens changes.
